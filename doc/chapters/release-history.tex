\chapter*{Release History}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Release History}

The entries below follow the version metadata and dated release commits kept in the project history.

\begin{description}[
  style=nextline, leftmargin=1.5em, labelindent=0pt, labelwidth=1em, labelsep=0.5em, itemindent=0pt, font=\normalfont\bfseries] \item[v0.4.3 --- July 17, 2026] Consolidated the introductory continuity and Bernstein material, added an upfront map of \code{pdmat}, \code{pdvar}, \code{rhodiff}, \code{pdlmi}, and the YALMIP solver boundary, and distinguished exact rate-vertex reduction from finite sufficient parameter-domain certificates. The desired cross-cell behaviour and monomial equality now appear before one degree-\(m\) Bernstein treatment and a unified explanation of physical cells, local labels, and nested coefficient storage.

  \item[v0.4.2 --- July 17, 2026] Reorganized the manual around the path from LPV models to cellwise constraints, highlighted the general DPD-LMI, and added a worked shared-face continuity/Lipschitz example.  Installation now documents the one-call installer and validation path; the certificate reference records the two-parameter Putinar and Full Box backend blocks.  The synchronized Web guide uses the same five-stage workflow.

  \item[v0.3.6 --- July 16, 2026] Completed the renamed \code{pdbase}/\code{pdmat}/\code{pdvar}/\code{pdlmi} reference, expanded cell-local Bernstein and solver examples, documented direct, P\'olya, Putinar, and full-box certificates, and refreshed the printable and Web plotting assets.  Released by commit \code{fe74a65}.

  \item[v0.2.12 --- July 13, 2026] Published the second complete manual and Web-reference milestone, including matrix-function plotting and algebra, derivative-aware decision expressions, finite LMI assembly, and the full-box certificate interface.  Released by commit \code{98b63e5}.

  \item[v0.1.0 --- July 3, 2026] Introduced the original grid-and-storage foundation and the first package manual, including Bernstein traversal, validation, and helper contracts. Released by commit \code{63c06ee}.
\end{description}
