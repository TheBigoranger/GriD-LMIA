\chapter*{Release History}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Release History}

The entries below follow the version metadata and dated release commits kept in the project history.

\begin{description}[
  style=nextline, leftmargin=1.5em, labelindent=0pt, labelwidth=1em, labelsep=0.5em, itemindent=0pt, font=\normalfont\bfseries] \item[v0.4.4 --- July 18, 2026] Consolidates the v0.4 release line: the manual now follows the LPV-model-to-finite-constraint workflow, explains continuity, Bernstein storage, rate vertices, and the implemented direct, P\'olya, Putinar, and Full Box selections in one coherent reference.  This refresh simplifies syntax and example listings, improves compact pagination, redraws the affected coefficient, indexing, and certificate diagrams, and synchronizes the printable and Web manuals.

  \item[v0.3.6 --- July 16, 2026] Completed the renamed \code{pdbase}/\code{pdmat}/\code{pdvar}/\code{pdlmi} reference, expanded cell-local Bernstein and solver examples, documented direct, P\'olya, Putinar, and full-box certificates, and refreshed the printable and Web plotting assets.  Released by commit \code{fe74a65}.

  \item[v0.2.12 --- July 13, 2026] Published the second complete manual and Web-reference milestone, including matrix-function plotting and algebra, derivative-aware decision expressions, finite LMI assembly, and the full-box certificate interface.  Released by commit \code{98b63e5}.

  \item[v0.1.0 --- July 3, 2026] Introduced the original grid-and-storage foundation and the first package manual, including Bernstein traversal, validation, and helper contracts. Released by commit \code{63c06ee}.
\end{description}
